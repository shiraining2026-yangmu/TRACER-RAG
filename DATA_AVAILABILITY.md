# Data and model availability

## Publicly available

Code and selected numerical supporting data are available at [TRACER-RAG on GitHub](https://github.com/shiraining2026-yangmu/TRACER-RAG).

- Source code, tests and symbolic nonmedical fixtures generated by `examples/offline_demo.py`.
- Aggregate Dev/Test reranking-search metrics and source hashes, without queries or evidence text.
- Archived per-question binary correctness for 1,138 exam items, eight models and three arms; primary 1,137-item paired statistics and an all-1,138 sensitivity analysis.
- 222 categorical structure-ablation assessments and 600 candidate-level reciprocal AI-score rows; archived strict-ablation, external-transfer and 111-query K-only/TER aggregate assessments.
- Identifier-only membership of 345 frozen evaluation queries and 3,000 Build seed records; frozen corpus counts and file hashes.
- A field dictionary, source/export hashes, protocol limitations and a script to check the numerical supporting records in [`reproducibility/supporting_data`](reproducibility/supporting_data/README.md).
- A source manifest and checksums for the publication package.

Run `python scripts/check_supporting_data.py` to validate the released CSVs, paired counts/exact tests, score summaries and membership boundaries without private data or model inference.

## Not redistributed

The A/K/E source corpora, clinical/source narratives, raw question/answer texts and answer keys, expert workbooks, teacher/SFT labels, individual text predictions, embeddings/indexes, model weights and trained adapters are not included. Their redistribution permissions, privacy review and model terms cannot be inferred from a local file's existence. No public download link for those artifacts is provided here, and this release does not promise that all private artifacts are available on request.

To run full experiments, obtain authorized source data and model files independently. Follow `docs/DATA_CONTRACTS.md`, preserve the Build/Dev/Test split, remove training/evaluation overlaps before training, and retain input/model/prompt hashes. The public membership CSVs contain identifiers and relation enums, not the underlying question or corpus text. Public benchmark source repositories and pinned revisions, where recorded, are listed in `external_transfer_aggregates.json`; their own terms apply. Corpus counts and manuscript results cannot be reconstructed from the synthetic demo.

## Evidence boundaries

Numerical records permit descriptive recalculation, not independent regrading or verification of clinical correctness. Some local records were recovered as aggregates only. The supporting package preserves unfavorable results and differences between historical versions. The weight-search tables remain aggregates identified by their input hashes, without per-query retrieval records.

Historical answer-key-selected wrong-only EviCR runs retained correct upstream answers. They are conditional repair diagnostics, not blind full-Test deployment effects. The strict-ablation archive uses this routing too. The known overlap `QB-2022-TCM-03-080` is excluded from primary paired scoring; an all-1,138 sensitivity set is retained. Scoring exclusion does not establish that the Critic was retrained without the overlapping record.

Automated evidence/open-QA ratings are exploratory model judgments, not independent expert or clinical assessments. External direct-transfer metrics do not measure frozen-evidence fidelity, and pretraining exposure to public benchmarks cannot be ruled out. The current q,D-only inference runner is provided separately; its presence does not establish a completed clean full-Test experiment for a particular adapter.

## Additional artifacts for an exact paper replication

A complete replication would additionally need the authorized frozen questions and corpus/index, exact environment/model revisions, trained q,D-only adapter, frozen text predictions and independent annotations. These artifacts are not supplied. The numerical package and synthetic demo do not replace missing experiments or medical results.

# Data and model availability

## Included

- Source code, tests and symbolic nonmedical fixtures generated by `examples/offline_demo.py`.
- Aggregate Dev/Test reranking-search metrics and source hashes, without queries or evidence text.
- A source manifest and checksums for the publication package.

## Not redistributed

The A/K/E corpora, clinical/source narratives, raw question banks and answer keys, expert workbooks, teacher/SFT labels, individual predictions, embeddings/indexes, model weights and trained adapters are not included. Their redistribution permissions, privacy review and model terms cannot be inferred from a local file's existence. There is no public download link for these private artifacts in this release.

To run full experiments, obtain authorized source data and model files independently. Follow `docs/DATA_CONTRACTS.md`, preserve the Build/Dev/Test split, remove training/evaluation overlaps before training, and retain input/model/prompt hashes. Corpus counts and manuscript results cannot be reconstructed from the synthetic demo.

## Evidence boundaries

The saved weight-search tables describe the archived experiment identified by their input hashes; they are not expert-validated clinical outcomes. No per-item records are published with which to independently recompute those aggregate metrics.

EviCR inference and fidelity-evaluation scripts are included. Their presence does not constitute a completed, independently verified fidelity result for a particular trained adapter. Do not turn historical answer-aware or answer-key-selected repair outputs into current full-Test results.

## Additional artifacts for an exact paper replication

A complete replication would additionally need the authorized frozen split/queries, retrieval corpus and index, exact environment/model revisions, trained q,D-only adapter, frozen predictions, independent annotations and paired correctness records. These are not supplied in this code release. No missing artifact is replaced by simulated medical results.
